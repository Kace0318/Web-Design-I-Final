Skyline's Chicken Tenders

Skyline's newest and most exciting edition to the menu

I guess it's obvious now where I live. However, I haven't always lived in Ohio.

For any non-Ohioans who aren't aware, Skyline is Cincinnati's signature style chili typically eaten on a Coney (hot-dog) or spaghetti, topped with onions, mustard, and a MOUNTAIN of cheese. There's nowhere in Ohio you can go where you won't a Skyline.

As a non-native Ohioan, I've had a love hate relationship with Skyline. While the flavors were good, the effect it had on my stomach was less than desirable. However, there is one menu item that constantly bring me back, and that is the Grilled Chicken Wrap. There's something about it I'm obsessed with.

So imagine my surprise when I pull up to the Skyline drive-thru ready to order my grilled chicken usual, when I see all new chicken tenders on the menu. At Skyline of all places!

So obviously, I had to give them a try.

The Review

$13.45 gets you a basket of 4 tenders and fries, no drink.

The tenders were really really crunchy and the chicken on the inside could have been seasoned a little better. They weren’t juicy nor were they dry, however, the thick crunchy breading on the outside is what contributed to the chicken’s dryness.

I feel like most of the flavor came from the sauce. And the sauce (chili ranch), was good. Bu this is a chicken tender review site. If most of the flavor of a tender comes from the dipping sauce, then you’ve made a good sauce, not a good tender.

With all that being said, I’m going to give them a 2.9/5. It’s certainly not the worst tender I’ve had, not even close, but it wasn’t amazing either. I can’t really see myself ordering these again, especially since it can't compete against my beloved grilled chicken wrap.

Is it wrong to judge Skyline so harshly for their chicken tenders being that they're known primarily for ground beef? Perhaps. However, when I see tenders to judge, then I will do so accordingly.
