Fear not picky eaters! For I am your humble guide to greatness!

Greatness being the best chicken tenders I can find.

If you're anything like me, eating new and unfamiliar foods can be risky.

Since I was young, eating something I ended up not liking could have been disastrous for me. I have always had a sensitive stomach and trying new foods was always risky. I'll spare you the grotesque details of the results.

However, the one thing that has never let me down is chicken tenders.

When I eat at a restaurant with my friends or family, I will order chicken tenders 90% of the time. Unless of course, it's not a menu item (unfortunately). At the end of 2019, I started to review chicken tenders as a fun hobby on Instagram. But for my entertainment, and hopefully yours too, I've decided to bring my interviews to a wider audience

If you're on this page, I think it's safe to assume that you're either:

  • a picky eater
  • a chicken tender enthusiast
  • all of the above

Nevertheless, you've made your way to Kaylee's Tendie Reviews where I, Kaylee Acevedo, will bring you honest reviews about my favorite (or not so favorite) tenders from various businesses that I visit.

As a picky eater, I pay attention to every detail about all foods, let alone chicken tenders. I will apply the same criteria to the chicken tenders I review too. Thanks for reading my reviews and I hope you find your next favorite tendie!
